COLLATERAL SECURITY Nil (Exempt)
REPAYMENT TENURE 3 to 7 Years

Eligibility Criteria

  • Any individual above 18 years of age.
  • Minimum 8th standard pass for manufacturing projects above ₹10 Lakhs and service projects above ₹5 Lakhs.
  • Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and registered institutions are also eligible.
  • No income ceiling for setting up new units.
